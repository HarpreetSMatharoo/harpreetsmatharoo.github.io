02 — HOW I WORK

From customer signal to measurable product change.

I bring customer context, technical understanding, and product judgment together to move from an ambiguous problem to a useful, trusted experience—and keep learning after launch.

A practical product workflow

Connected steps, with evidence and clear decisions at each stage.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the work

Listen to customers and customer-facing teams. Map the real task, recurring friction, and the constraints around it.

Useful outputs User needs · Workflow map · Evidence of the problem

02 / FRAME

Choose the right problem

Turn what you learn into an opportunity, a clear outcome, and a focused priority. Make assumptions and trade-offs visible.

Useful outputs Problem brief · Product options · Success measures

03 / SHAPE

Design for usefulness and trust

Define the experience and requirements, including data and access boundaries, failure paths, human review, and quality checks.

Useful outputs Requirements · Guardrails · Evaluation approach

04 / DELIVER & LEARN

Ship in steps; learn from use

Align teams on scope and readiness. Roll out deliberately, then use adoption, quality, and feedback signals to guide what changes next.

Useful outputs Roadmap and backlog · Release plan · Learning loop
